How we work

Verified source

We recompile each contract from its published source and accept it only when the result matches the deployed bytecode exactly.

From the chain

Every answer starts with on-chain events and state, not someone else's summary. Every read is kept, so any answer can be traced back.

Checked

What we derive is checked against the contracts' own getters, with the evidence for each field.

Played forward

On a fork of mainnet, the people we name can make the call and everyone else is refused. A queued change fails a second before the time we give, and goes through at it.

Fresh

Refreshed from the chain every few minutes. Each answer carries the block it was read at, so you know exactly how current it is.

Pay per answer

No accounts or subscriptions. Agents and people pay for the call they make.
